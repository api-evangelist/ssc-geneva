The environment surrounding alternative investment fund accounting is evolving in ways fund managers can no longer afford to ignore. Heightened regulatory scrutiny, increasingly complex asset classes and rising expectations for data quality are reshaping how audits are planned, executed and evaluated. At the same time, rapid advances in technology are forcing a sharper look at where automation can add value—and where human oversight remains essential for protecting the integrity of financial reporting.
