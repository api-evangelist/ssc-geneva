Private credit has grown considerably in scale and complexity over the past decade, and the data infrastructure supporting it has struggled to keep pace. Loan data remains one of the most operationally intensive areas in the asset class, as large volumes of data arrive in formats that make automation challenging. Agent notices, credit agreements and amendment documents come in as PDFs, emails and spreadsheets, each structured differently, and requiring someone to read, interpret and extract the information that matters.
