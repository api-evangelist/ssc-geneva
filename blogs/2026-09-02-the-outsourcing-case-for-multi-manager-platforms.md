Multi-manager hedge fund platforms face a structural tension that only intensifies as they grow. The model demands operational breadth, combining multiple portfolio managers, strategies, asset classes and geographies under a single framework. But that breadth creates complexity, and complexity eventually creates operational strain and cost.
