<p>In today's fixed income markets, voluntary corporate actions (VCA) have outgrown their reputation as routine administrative tasks. Tender offers, exchange offers, consent solicitations and distressed restructurings now come with cross-border asset structures, multi-currency options, early participation incentives and issuer deadlines that leave little room for error. Investment teams aren't just asking whether they can participate, they're asking whether they can participate accurately, quickly and with confidence.</p>
