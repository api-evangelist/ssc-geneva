The first half of 2026 served as a pivotal maturing point for private credit. Despite the continued strength and long-term appeal of the asset class, the early months of this year introduced the industry to its first systemic shock. Between delayed corporate repayments and a tightening of bank-led leverage facilities, fund managers were forced to navigate a sudden, sharp demand for liquidity from institutional LPs.
