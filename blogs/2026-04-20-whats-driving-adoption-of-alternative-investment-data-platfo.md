<p>As alternative investments shift from a niche allocation to a core component of portfolios, firms are finding that the primary challenge is no longer access to private markets, but rather, managing them efficiently and transparently over time. Across the alternative investment landscape, adoption of modern document and data management platforms is being driven by mounting operational pressure that is reshaping how wealth managers, institutional investors and operations teams think about alternatives infrastructure.</p>
