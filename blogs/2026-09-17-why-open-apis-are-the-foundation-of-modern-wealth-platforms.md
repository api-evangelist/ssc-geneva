In today's market, connectivity is becoming the defining measure of a wealth platform's value. Wealth management firms rely on a growing ecosystem of CRM systems, client portals, planning tools, business intelligence platforms and, increasingly, AI-enabled applications. As this ecosystem expands, firms need technology partners that can integrate seamlessly with the tools they already use, enabling them to build advice journeys that are efficient, scalable and tailored to the needs of their clients.
