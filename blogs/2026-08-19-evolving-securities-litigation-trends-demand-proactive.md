The first half of 2026 has reinforced a reality that institutional investors have been experiencing for several years: securities class action litigation continues to evolve in both volume and complexity. While the headlines often focus on record settlements or high-profile cases, the broader trends reveal a more important story that underscores the growing need for sophisticated monitoring, analytics and claims administration.
