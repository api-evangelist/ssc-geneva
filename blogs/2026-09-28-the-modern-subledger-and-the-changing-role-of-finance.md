The subledger has traditionally been viewed as a supporting accounting function, providing granular detail and helping finance teams reconcile the general ledger. That view increasingly understates its role. A modern, purpose-built subledger can bring together a bank’s financial data, support a more continuous close process and give finance teams a stronger foundation for analysis and decision-making.
