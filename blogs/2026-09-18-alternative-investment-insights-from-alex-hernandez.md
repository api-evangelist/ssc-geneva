As alternative investments become more accessible to a wider range of investors, advisors are facing growing demands around transparency, reporting accuracy and operational efficiency. Alex Hernandez, Director of SS&C Accord, works with clients navigating these challenges, combining automation and specialized oversight to streamline the management of alternative investment data. In this Q&A, Hernandez shares her perspective on the market forces driving growth in the sector and how technology is transforming support for investors while driving greater operational efficiencies.
