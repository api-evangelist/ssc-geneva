For a life insurer, few levers matter as much as capital efficiency. A single change in how an asset is treated for risk-based capital (RBC) can lift the return on required capital across an entire allocation. That is precisely what has happened for residential mortgage loans (RMLs) held in fund structures, and it deserves the attention of any insurer or asset manager active in the space.
