Financial markets have remained resilient throughout 2026. Despite ongoing geopolitical tension and mounting talk of an AI-driven asset bubble, the Cboe Volatility Index (VIX) has drifted to multi-month lows while CDX credit default swap indexes spreads remain compressed. By the numbers, investors are not worried.
