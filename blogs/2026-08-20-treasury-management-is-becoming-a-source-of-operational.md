Institutional investors have grown more discerning about where they place capital. Strong returns and a solid track record are no longer enough on their own. Allocators now scrutinize how fund managers handle concentration risk, counterparty exposure, fee transparency, borrowing costs and margin requirements, treating the inner workings of fund operations as a core part of due diligence.
