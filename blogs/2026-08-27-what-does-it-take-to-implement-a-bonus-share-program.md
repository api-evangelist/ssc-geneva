Bonus share programs are gaining traction as asset managers look for new ways to attract and retain investors in an increasingly competitive retail alternatives market. Interval funds and tender offer funds have found these programs to be an effective mechanism for rewarding investor loyalty while differentiating their offerings from a growing field of competitors. The concept itself is straightforward.
