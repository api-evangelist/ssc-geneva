Asset managers are looking for new sources of capital, but reaching a broader investor base can introduce a new set of operational demands. Private market strategies are attracting interest from investors who historically have had limited access, creating an opportunity for managers to expand beyond their traditional institutional base. The challenge is doing so without adding an operating model that becomes too complex or costly to manage.
