<p style="line-height: 20px;"><span>Bank loans and private credit have now become staple investments within most modern insurance portfolios. As insurers pursue yield, diversification and better risk-adjusted returns, these instruments offer compelling advantages over traditional fixed income investments. At the same time, they introduce a level of accounting and operational complexity that many existing systems are not properly equipped to handle.</span></p>
