A property close can appear complete long before every risk has been identified. Financial statements have been finalized, reconciliations have been reviewed, and reporting deadlines have been met. Then an audit inquiry uncovers an unsupported balance, a significant change is questioned or details of a capital expenditure reveal an issue that should have been identified weeks earlier.
