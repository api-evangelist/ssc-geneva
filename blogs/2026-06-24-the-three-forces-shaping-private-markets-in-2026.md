Private markets in 2026 are being shaped by three converging forces: artificial intelligence, interest rate uncertainty, political tensions and persistent illiquidity challenges. These forces aren’t new, but the way they are interacting is creating a more complex operating environment than most managers anticipated heading into the year. The firms navigating it most effectively are building strategies around conditions as they exist.
