Loan data problems are easy to identify at the point where they surface, such as a delayed report, an error caught during reconciliation or a covenant metric that does not match what the borrower submitted. The initiation of the process breakdown is harder to visualize. For most private credit operations, data integrity issues are not isolated incidents.
