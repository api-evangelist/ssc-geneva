Retail participation is expected to play a larger role in the future of private markets. For APAC secondaries managers, the key question is whether existing infrastructure can support the operational demands that come with a broader investor base. The operational implications are significant, particularly in a region characterized by diverse regulatory frameworks, reporting requirements and distribution models.
