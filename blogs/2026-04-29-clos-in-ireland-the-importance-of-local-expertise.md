<p><span style="line-height: 20.85px;">Collateralized Loan Obligations (CLOs) have become a core component of institutional credit markets. For asset managers and private markets investors seeking diversified exposure to leveraged loans, CLO structures offer a well-established, scalable and transparent mechanism for deploying capital. At the center of this market sits Ireland, which has become one of the leading global domiciles for CLO vehicles and the service infrastructure that supports them.</span></p>
